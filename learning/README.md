# Learning route

Start with [the story](story.md): two hypotheses can have the same mean loss while a tilted objective prefers one, and the accuracy required of that preference determines which repeated contexts the source must supply.

The project uses one educational anchor: John Watrous, *The Theory of Quantum Information* (2018). [The anchor and notation bridge](anchor.md) identifies the relevant sections and connects states, measurements and distinguishability to this learning model. It also identifies the classical approximation and collision arguments supplied locally rather than covered by the book.

[The common 30-milestone map](milestones.md) records the understanding targets and their dependencies. Its 12 background milestones assemble the model; its 18 narrative milestones connect the example, proof mechanisms, theorem and resource interpretation. The map is a guide to the project, not a requirement to read the entire anchor first.
